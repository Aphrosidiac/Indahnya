## SEO footprint

[measured] 12 November 2026.

- robots.txt: allow all, blocks dashboard, pay, auth, card instance paths. https://ekadinvite.my/robots.txt
- sitemap.xml: 116 URLs, BM and EN pairs. 14 `/galeri` plus 14 `/gallery` design pages by event type, 8 `/panduan` plus 8 `/guides`, event-type pages (`semua-majlis-acara`), features, harga, how-it-works, reviews (`/ulasan`), terms.
- Pattern: one landing page per event type and one guide set. A model for Indahnya's "per event" pages if it wants aqiqah, tunang and birthday coverage.

## Weaknesses

- No guest photo upload seen (gallery is 10 host photos on Gold). [measured]
- No seat finder, voice wishes or TV slideshow seen. [measured]
- A generous refund window is a trust point they own.

## Where Indahnya can honestly beat them

1. Everything after the invitation: guest photos, TV slideshow, seat finder, voice wishes.
2. Not honest to claim: cheaper e-kad (RM29 vs RM59) or more designs (325+). EkadInvite is ahead on invitation SEO and on its refund window (48 hours; Indahnya's event-day promise in `/bayaran-balik` (see facts.md; not re-read for the truth file) is different, so compare carefully).
