# Teardown: the "do-nothing" alternatives (WhatsApp group, Google Drive, Google Photos, photographer only)

Checked 12 November 2026. These are free or already paid for, so they are the real competitor for many Malaysian couples. The honest job is to say when they are enough.

**Labels.** [measured] fetched today. [sourced] the vendor's own help page, URL given, checked 12 November 2026. [third-party] others' claims. [inferred] my reading. [unverified] could not confirm.

## 1. WhatsApp group

**What it is.** The default. Guests are added to a group, or the couple says "hantar dalam group ya", and people send photos after the majlis.

**Facts from the vendor.**
- WhatsApp offers Standard and HD quality for each send, and HD can be made the default under Settings > Storage and data > Media upload quality. [sourced: https://faq.whatsapp.com/759301289012856/]
- A photo already sent cannot be upgraded; it must be sent again in HD. [sourced: same page]
- HD is clearer than Standard but uses more data and takes longer; connection quality affects the result even with HD. [sourced: same page]
- The page does not describe the compression method or give file sizes. [measured via WebFetch summary]
- Search summary of a WhatsApp help result: "media files are usually compressed when shared". [third-party summary of the vendor's page; not confirmed on the page I fetched]

**Why couples use it.** Free, everyone has it, no new tool to explain. Kenangan's own page agrees it is fine for a small majlis where everyone is already in one group. [third-party: https://kenanganmajlis.com/banding/galeri-qr-vs-whatsapp]

**Pain (inferred and third-party).**
- Photos mixed with replies, links and voice notes. [third-party: Kenangan comparison]
- Each guest decides the quality, and many do not set HD. [inferred from the vendor page above]
- Nobody sends: Kenang's page quotes the line "Nanti aku send", tapi tak pernah sampai. [sourced as vendor marketing copy: https://kenang.my/; a rival's wording, not a buyer's]
- A kenduri guest list can be hundreds of people who do not know each other, so a single group is awkward [inferred].

**How Indahnya can honestly win.** Original-quality upload kept in one place with a download-all; a QR on the table so guests do not need to be added to anything; a TV slideshow. Say it is a fit for medium and large majlis, and that WhatsApp is fine for a small gathering. Do not claim WhatsApp "destroys" quality; the vendor page does not say that.

## 2. Google Drive folder

**Facts.**
- Link sharing: with "Anyone with the link", anyone can open the file without signing in to a Google Account. [sourced via search summary of Google Drive Help: https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494822]
- Editor role can add new files. Whether a signed-out visitor can upload to a shared folder is not stated in the pages found. [unverified; the brief's `owner-todo.md` already marks this "NEEDS SOURCE"]
- Free storage is 15 GB shared across Drive, Gmail and Photos. Paid tiers shown for Malaysia in US dollars: 100 GB US$1.99 a month, 2 TB US$9.99. [sourced via search summary: https://one.google.com/about/plans?hl=en-MY]
- Workspace admins can restrict external link sharing. [sourced via search summary]

**Pain (inferred).** Free but clunky on a phone for older guests; no slideshow; no table card with a short link; the host's own Google storage is used; guests may be asked to sign in [unverified].

**How Indahnya can honestly win.** Guests never sign in or install anything [truth file]; hosts do sign in. A printed QR is simpler than explaining a Drive link. Be careful: do not say Drive requires a Google account for uploading until it is tested with a signed-out browser.

## 3. Google Photos shared album

**Facts.**
- The album owner decides whether others can add photos, comments and likes. [sourced: https://support.google.com/photos/answer/9789702]
- Sharing with specific people is tied to Google Accounts. The help page does not say whether someone opening a link needs one. [sourced + [unverified]]
- Google Photos can generate a QR code for an album. [third-party: Kenangan's page says so, https://kenanganmajlis.com/banding/galeri-qr-vs-google-photos]
- Album item limits: 20,000 items per album in 2017 to 2018 news reports; the current number is not on the help page I read. [third-party, old]

**How Indahnya can honestly win.** Dedicated majlis flow: e-kad, RSVP, seat finder, TV slideshow, wishes. Be honest that Google Photos can already do QR sharing and is good for long-term storage; Kenangan itself suggests using both.

## 4. Photographer only

**What it is.** Many Malaysian couples rely on the hired photographer and videographer and take no guest photos.

**Evidence.**
- A viral photographer in Malaysia gave guests a QR code to download photos taken on the pelamin straight away. SAYS reported ~280K views on TikTok on 22 September 2023. Commenters called it "Another level" and said local photographers did not do this yet. [third-party: https://says.com/my/seismik/video-scan-terus-dapat-gambar-atas-pelamin-ramai-puji-cara-kreatif-photographer-ini] Shows the idea is already admired, and that photographers can offer it.
- Photobooth hire is a related spend. "Photobooth kahwin" has heavy Google autocomplete (harga, murah, sewa, diy) [measured]. One rival says rentals cost RM400 to RM1,150. [third-party claim by Gathrly, unverified]
- Kenangan's page "galeri tetamu vs jurugambar kahwin" and POV and Wedibox blog posts argue guest photos complement, not replace, the photographer. [third-party]

**How Indahnya can honestly win.** Position as the second camera for spontaneous moments. Do not say it replaces the photographer. Possible partner channel: photographers and vendors (offsite-plan.md already lists vendors).

## Where "do nothing" wins and Indahnya should say so

- A small majlis with 30 guests in one WhatsApp group.
- A couple who already pay for Google storage and do not need a slideshow or RSVP.
- A couple who will not print a QR card.
