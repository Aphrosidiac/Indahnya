# Teardown: smaller Malaysian e-kad and invitation platforms (Jemputan.me, Nak Jemput, KadKami, tetamu.me, Raikad)

Checked 12 November 2026. Grouped because each is small or a thin site. Tuan Majlis, EkadInvite and Neekah Kita have their own files. Also seen in a Raikad URL list but not checked: SayaKahwin, KahwinNow (Raikad has "vs" pages against them, Canva and Google Form).

**Labels.** [measured] fetched today. [sourced] vendor's own page, URL given, checked 12 November 2026. [third-party] others' claims. [inferred] my reading. [unverified] could not confirm.

## Jemputan.me

- Pricing is per card or link. Packages (read in a browser; plain HTML is empty, the site is a JavaScript app): Bronze RM30, Silver RM40, Gold RM60 per card. Add-ons: upload your own design +RM10, upload a video cover +RM10. [sourced: https://jemputan.me/packages]
- The feature icons per tier did not come through as text, so what separates Bronze from Gold is [unverified]. Feature names listed: calendar, contacts, countdown, navigation, song, effects, attendance, RSVP/wish, guest list, photo gallery, money gift, wishlist, custom link, live preview. "All features are optional."
- Self-serve "Studio" editor, tutorial, FAQ, vendor "Partner program". Language switch: English and Melayu. [measured]
- SEO: 10 URLs (catalog, packages, studio, tutorial, faq, vendor-program, legal). Title is the same on every page: "Jemputan | Kad Kahwin Digital". [measured] Weak server-side rendering means little indexable text for search engines [inferred: the HTML body is 5 KB].
- Google autocomplete shows "jemputan.me login" and "jemputan.me free" [measured].
- No guest gallery upload seen.

## Nak Jemput (nakjemput.com)

- Operator: Khairulzul Enterprise (JR0158926-V), Melaka. [sourced: footer]
- Prices: Basic RM27.90 (info editable 90 days; contact, calendar, countdown, music, gallery, location), Premium RM57.90 (180 days; adds YouTube video, money gift, RSVP, wishlist, guestbook, Melayu/English, custom design, PDF beta). "Link tak expire". [sourced: https://nakjemput.com/pakej]
- Claims "1,000+ pelanggan berpuas hati", "278+ design". Testimonials are labelled Shopee reviews: the shop sells through Shopee as well. [sourced; [unverified]]
- Also a reseller program. [sourced: nav]
- SEO: sitemap lists 2 URLs (home and terms). robots blocks admin, panel, reseller, api. [measured] Title: "Kad Kahwin & Jemputan Digital Online Murah | Nak Jemput".
- No guest gallery upload seen.

## KadKami (kadkami.com)

- Status: "Coming Soon". The page offers "personal request" custom cards by email, limited to 5 slots a month, one design shown. [sourced: https://kadkami.com/]
- No sitemap (404). robots.txt is Cloudflare's default content-signal text. [measured]
- Not a competitor today. Do not write a comparison page.

## tetamu.me

- Cards: Classic RM39, Signature RM59 (adds RSVP and Salam Kaut), Royal RM89 (adds Gift Reservation and two languages). [sourced: https://tetamu.me/packages]
- "Digital Guest Gallery": photos, voice recordings and wishes via QR or link, RM99 per album, no app. [sourced: https://tetamu.me/album]
- SEO: 8 URLs. [measured] A very small site, but it pairs invitation and gallery, like Indahnya, and uses the same RM99 gallery price.

## Raikad (raikad.com)

- Free e-kad: "100 reka bentuk, RM0 buat masa ini, tanpa kad bank". [sourced: https://raikad.com/]
- Guest memories feature: free for now, 20 photos and 5 voice notes per event, photos and voice notes private to the host. [sourced: https://raikad.com/en/event-photo-sharing]
- SEO: 247 URLs: about 200 `/templates`, 10 `/panduan`, 8 `/ciri`, 4 `/vs` pages (sayakahwin, kahwinnow, canva, google-form), BM and EN. [measured: https://raikad.com/sitemap.xml]
- Why it matters: "free" is the dominant intent in e-kad search (see the market file). A free competitor with comparison pages sets the price anchor at RM0.
- Weakness: the free limit "buat masa ini" (for now) is not a promise; photo and voice caps are small. [sourced]

## Where Indahnya can honestly beat them

1. The group is invitation-first. Guest photos, TV slideshow and seat finder are mostly missing; only tetamu.me and Raikad have a photo or voice feature, and Raikad's is capped at 20.
2. Retention and caps are not stated on tetamu.me's page [unverified]; Indahnya can publish exact numbers.
3. Not honest to claim: cheaper or better-designed invitations. Prices start at RM0 (Raikad), RM27.90 (Nak Jemput), RM30 (Jemputan.me).
