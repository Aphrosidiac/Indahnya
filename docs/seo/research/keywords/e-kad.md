## Summary

1. This is the biggest typed demand in the whole research. Autocomplete alphabet expansion gave 93 distinct completions for `kad kahwin digital`, 87 for
   `kad jemputan kahwin online`, 51 for `kad jemputan digital`, 48 for `kad jemputan aqiqah` [measured].
2. It is also the most crowded. Every top-10 had 8 to 10 dedicated Malaysian e-kad vendors, plus Canva, CapCut and Shopee [measured, D]. At least 40 distinct
   vendor domains appeared across 12 queries (raikad.com, raikan.com.my, ekad.akad.com.my, ekadinvite.my, jemputan.me, e-card.my, thekahwin.my, sayakahwin.com,
   kadkawindigital.my, lakarra.my, nakjemput.com, kad2u.com, reception.my, evently.my, invitee.my and more).
3. [inferred] A new domain will not win the head terms (`kad kahwin digital`, `e kad kahwin percuma`, `template kad kahwin digital`). It can win narrow
   queries where Indahnya's bundle is the answer: e-kad that also has the photo gallery, the table finder and ucapan in one link.
4. Free is the hook of the head: `percuma` and `free` are in the top completions. Indahnya's e-kad is part of RM0/RM59/RM99, not a separate free kad. Do not
   claim "kad percuma" unless the Percuma plan includes the kad (truth file: "Semua ciri ada dalam pakej percuma" is true, so a free kad exists with limits on uploads and time, not features).
5. The site is pre-launch. Sign-up is closed (`/mula`). A page can describe and show the sample kad (`/contoh/kad` is noindex today). It must not say "buat kad sekarang" as if it works.
