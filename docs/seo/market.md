## 1. The short version

1. **The category has no common BM name yet.** BM gallery searches such as "galeri gambar majlis", "qr gambar kahwin" and "cara kumpul gambar kahwin" return no Google autocomplete, while e-kad searches return many ("kad kahwin digital free", "rsvp kahwin google form") [measured]. So either demand is small or buyers have not found words for it yet. Both are [inferred]. The safe plan: win e-kad and RSVP intent first, and teach the gallery inside it.
2. **The field is crowded and young.** At least ten Malaysian players sell a QR gallery or e-kad plus gallery, most launched in 2026 [inferred from SSM numbers and page dates]. The brief named one (Kenangan). I found Kenang, momenspace, povkita, Gathrly, JomGambar, Neekah Kita and tetamu.me as well. See `competitors.md`.
3. **Indahnya's clearest edge is retention and caps.** Rivals keep galleries 7 to 90 days (Kenangan 2 months) and cap photos at 500 to 10,000 or 20 shots per guest. Indahnya keeps media 1 or 2 years with no photo cap on paid plans [truth file, `plans.ts`]. Say it plainly, with the exact numbers.
4. **Indahnya is not the cheapest, and must not say so.** Rival entry prices run from RM15 (povkita promo) to RM49. Indahnya's paid plan is RM59 [sourced].
5. **The bundle is real but others are coming for it.** Neekah Kita sells card plus gallery (RM29 or RM49 card, free or RM99 gallery). tetamu.me sells cards plus a RM99 album. Kenangan sells gallery plus e-kad and RSVP at RM65 [sourced].

## 3. Pains (what the page should name)

Ranked by how much evidence exists. Details in `research/market/couples-and-hosts.md`.

1. **Photos stay on guests' phones.** The WhatsApp request goes unanswered. Rivals' hero copy centres on it [sourced]; the SAYS viral QR clip shows public interest [third-party].
2. **WhatsApp quality and clutter.** HD is optional per send [sourced: WhatsApp help]. Photos mix with chat [third-party].
3. **Headcount and catering.** Over- and under-booking is a recurring news and blog topic [third-party]. Google Form is the default RSVP tool [measured: autocomplete].
4. **Photos disappear after a short period.** A gap in the rivals' product (2 months at Kenangan; 7 to 90 days elsewhere) [sourced].
5. **Many tools for one majlis:** kad in one app, RSVP in a form, photos in WhatsApp, money QR elsewhere [inferred].
6. **Cost stacking.** Add-ons everywhere: Kenangan RM35 plus RM30 plus RM10 [sourced].

## 4. Jobs to be done

[inferred] unless noted.

- Get every photo and video the guests took, in good quality, in one place, without chasing.
- Show guest photos live on the dewan screen.
- Send one link that holds the kad, map, RSVP and money QR.
- Know who is coming and how many, before the caterer needs the number.
- Help guests find their table (only where tables are assigned).
- Collect doa and wishes, including a voice note from a parent.
- Still have all of it in a year.

## 6. Objections with honest answers

| Objection | Honest answer for copy |
|---|---|
| "WhatsApp group kan percuma" | True. For a small gathering it is enough. A QR gallery helps when guests are many and do not know each other. Offer Percuma (RM0, 50 uploads) so they can test |
| "Mahal" | Do not claim cheapest. Show exactly what RM59 and RM99 include and how long photos are kept. Put it beside the cost of a photobooth only if that figure has a source |
| "Gambar hilang selepas majlis?" | State retention in plain numbers: kept 30 days (Percuma), 365 days (RM59), 730 days (RM99), counted from the majlis day [truth file] |
| "Orang tua tak reti scan" | Admit the risk. Offer a printed QR card and a link. Do not promise universal usage |
| "Privasi / gambar aurat" | List the actual controls the truth file confirms: couple pages noindex, location data removed from shown copies, originals private, approval mode. The truth file does not list a guest passcode, so do not mention one |
| "Line dewan teruk" | Needs an answer grounded in the code. Do not guess |
| "Brand baru, tak kenal" | Say who runs it (FF Dev Studio, address on pages) and how to reach a human on WhatsApp. No reviews or counts until real |
| "Kad digital percuma ada" | True (Raikad is RM0 for now) [sourced]. Position as the part after the invitation |

## 8. What this means for SEO and copy

All [inferred] from the evidence above.

1. **Lead with intent that has proven typing.** "kad kahwin digital" plus RSVP is the busy lane, led by "free". A landing page should be honest that Indahnya has a free tier (Percuma) and say exactly what it limits (50 uploads, 30 days, per facts.md).
2. **Teach the gallery as a named thing.** Use "galeri gambar tetamu" and "QR gambar majlis" in definitions, headings and FAQ so a first-time searcher finds the phrase. The JSON-LD already carries one-line definitions (facts.md).
3. **Own the retention claim, with numbers.** A page or table "berapa lama gambar disimpan": Percuma 30 days, RM59 1 year, RM99 2 years, against typical market ranges described as "7 to 90 days on the sites checked on 12 November 2026". Do not name a rival without a dated, sourced cell.
4. **Show the bundle as one link.** E-kad, RSVP, seat finder, wishes, TV slideshow, gallery. Neekah Kita and Kenangan claim parts of it, and the truth file confirms all of Indahnya's modules are built (e-kad, gambar, ucapan, rsvp, tempat). Sign-up is closed in pre-launch, so a page cannot promise an instant start.
5. **Pages by majlis type**, following Kenang (kahwin, tunang, aqiqah, konvokesyen, korporat) and EkadInvite (14 types), but only where there is search signal and real fit: nikah/akad, sanding/resepsi, tunang, majlis bertandang or second majlis, aqiqah.
6. **Answer the WhatsApp and Google objections on their own pages.** Kenangan already has `galeri-qr-vs-whatsapp` and `galeri-qr-vs-google-photos`. A better page is honest about when the free option is enough. See `competitors.md` for the rules.
7. **Language.** BM first, colloquial but clear ("tetamu scan QR, gambar masuk"), with an EN version. Rivals' BM is mixed formal and "awak". Avoid invented claims about hours, response times or promises the client did not state.
8. **Use no social proof until it exists.** No "ramai pasangan", no star ratings, no "100+ majlis". Rivals' counts (Kenang "19++ majlis", Nak Jemput "1,000+") are their own unverified claims.
9. **Both bots and humans.** Wedibox's `robots.txt` allows AI crawlers on marketing pages and blocks only couple pages [measured]. Indahnya's own decision on crawler policy is open (`owner-todo.md`); couple pages must stay noindex either way.
10. **Measure what matters.** No GSC, no logs yet. Do not promise rankings.
