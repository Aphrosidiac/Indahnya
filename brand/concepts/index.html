Indahnya logo,
four directions.

Each one is tied to something true about the product: the QR, Malay ornament, the moment people say "indahnya!", and the kad. All keep the brand green and ink, and the display face used on the new landing (Bricolage Grotesque), set lowercase.

Today: a green tile with a four-petal bloom. Clean, but it could be any app; nothing in it says photos, QR or majlis.

01

Mekar The QR that blooms

The product in one shape: three corners of a QR code, and where the fourth should be, a flower. Scan, and it turns into something beautiful. It also carries today's bloom forward, so it reads as an evolution, not a restart.

02

Titik Kerawang The i, dotted with a carved flower

A wordmark first. The dot of the i becomes a four-petal flower from Malay kerawang wood carving, the motif on pelamin, doors and kad borders. The mark on its own is just that i.

04

Mohor The seal on the kad

My pick: 01 Mekar, with 02's flower as the dot.

Mekar is the only one that explains the product at 16px: a QR that turns into a flower. It also grows out of today's bloom, so nothing we've built looks wrong. I'd use it as the icon and app mark, and set the wordmark "indahnya" with Titik Kerawang's carved flower as the dot of the i, which brings in the majlis warmth the QR alone lacks. Mohor stays useful as a print seal and kad badge, not as the main logo.
