# Teardown: global QR guest-photo apps (GuestPix, POV, GuestCam, Kululu)

Checked 12 November 2026. These are the names English-speaking buyers compare. None has a Malay site or MYR pricing that I could find. Wedibox has its own file.

**Labels.** [measured] fetched today. [sourced] vendor's own page, URL given, checked 12 November 2026. [third-party] others' claims. [inferred] my reading. [unverified] could not confirm.

## GuestPix (guestpix.com)

- Australian, "Proudly Australian made and owned". Not guestpix.co: that domain is a parked Namecheap page [measured].
- Prices, wedding, USD, one time [sourced: https://guestpix.com/weddings-pricing/]:

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 50 photos, unlimited guests, digital invitation and RSVPs, 30 day upload window |
| Classic | $49 | 1 gallery, unlimited photos and videos, content moderation, slideshow, written and video guestbook, 3 month upload and 12 month hosting |
| Signature | $89 | Custom theme and fonts, unlimited Super Albums, 12 month upload and hosting |
| Signature Bundle | $119 | 3 galleries, 3 QR codes, 24 month upload and hosting from purchase date |

- Currencies: USD, AUD, GBP, CAD, EUR, INR. No MYR. [measured]
- Prices load in the browser, so plain HTML fetches miss them. [sourced: Wedibox makes the same point; I confirmed it by reading the page in a browser]
- Claims: "150K+ global events", "over 100 countries", 4.8 from 46 reviews on the pricing page. [sourced; [unverified]]
- Languages in footer: EN, ES, FR, DE, IT, JA. No Malay. [measured]
- SEO: WordPress with All in One SEO. Sub-sitemaps: 92 pages, 75 posts, 59 landing pages, 22 gift guides. About 250 URLs [measured]. robots.txt lists AI bots (ClaudeBot, GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended) in a block; I read only the first lines, so whether they are blocked is [unverified].
- Weakness: no MYR, no BM, seat finder not listed (Wedibox claim, not GuestPix's own; [third-party]). Their Free plan has a 30-day window and 50 photos, the same shape as Indahnya's Free.

## POV (pov.camera)

- A "digital disposable camera" model: guests get a limited number of shots and the photos reveal later. iOS and Android app, plus an App Clip and web flow. [sourced: https://pov.camera/]
- Title: "QR Code Photo Sharing for Guests at Weddings, Parties & Events | POV" [measured].
- Price: a calculator by guest count. Free up to 10 guests. For up to 100 guests I read $34.99 USD, with a "business essentials" add-on (custom branding, live slideshow, 24/7 support) at +$20. [sourced: https://pov.camera/pricing, read in a browser]
- Extras: printed photobook, an NFC-style wooden block that opens the App Clip. [sourced: https://pov.camera/]
- Ratings claim: "4.9 stars, 28,433 ratings". [sourced; [unverified]]
- SEO: sitemap 29 URLs: 14 use-cases, 7 blog, 2 engineering, pricing, photobooks, voicemail, business, careers. [measured]
- Weakness: no BM, USD only, live slideshow behind an add-on, shot limits are a different product idea from "unlimited". No RSVP, seat finder or e-kad on its pages [measured: not seen].
- Third-party: a search summary found almost no independent complaints; its own blog says a competitor needs a guest app. [third-party]

## GuestCam (guestcam.co)

- QR gallery plus audio guestbook ("PhoneBooth"), AI photo finder ("MagicFind"), live slideshow. [sourced: https://guestcam.co/]
- Price: Standard $49 (struck from $55) one time, 6 month upload and 12 month storage, unlimited guests and photos. Premium $97 (struck from $110). [sourced: https://guestcam.co/pricing]
- Claim: "20M+ Memories Captured". [sourced; [unverified]]
- SEO: sitemap 52 URLs: 27 blog, 6 photo-sharing, 3 versus (guestpix, kululu, index), 2 M&A, 2 audio-guestbook, reviews, security. [measured]
- Weakness for Malaysia: USD, English. [measured]

## Kululu (kululu.com)

- Event photo wall and album, "Get started for free, pay only for extras". 50% off banner. [sourced: https://www.kululu.com/pricing]
- Plan prices were not captured from the page text. [unverified]
- Money-back "Fair Refund Policy" and a "Kululu for Professionals" line. [sourced]
- SEO: 41 URLs: 8 blog, 7 app static pages, a wedding-photo-sharing-app page, an alternative page. [measured]
- Weakness for Malaysia: English only, [measured].

## Where Indahnya can honestly beat them

1. Local fit: BM, ringgit, majlis types, local payment (FPX and DuitNow QR are the last confirmed methods; cards and e-wallets are not claimable yet [truth file]).
2. Bundle: e-kad, RSVP, seat finder, voice wishes under RM100. GuestPix does RSVP via invites; Wedibox does most of it at $79; GuestCam and POV are photo-led.
3. Free tier on par with GuestPix and Wedibox, so no loss there.
4. Not honest to claim: more mature, more reviews, or lower price per feature than these in all cases. POV at $34.99 for 100 guests is cheap, and the global names have large review counts.
