## Kenang (kenang.my)

- Concept: guests get a disposable-camera experience in the browser: 20 shots each, a film filter and orange date stamp, the original is kept as well. [sourced: https://kenang.my/]
- Also: voice notes (up to 30 s), written wishes, Salam Kaut (DuitNow QR), moderation before photos show, lock gallery until a chosen time, ZIP of everything.
- Positioning: "Setiap tetamu anda ialah photographer." Meta: "Bayar sekali RM99." [measured]
- Payment: "FPX atau DuitNow QR". [sourced]
- Proof: "Lebih 19++ majlis dah guna Kenang" plus testimonials with order numbers. [sourced; [unverified]]
- Refund: full refund if the event is cancelled and the QR was not used. [sourced: https://kenang.my/refund, partial read]
- SEO: sitemap 18 URLs, BM and `/en` pairs: home, kahwin, tunang, aqiqah, konvokesyen, korporat, feedback, legal, demo. Page per event type is the pattern to watch. [measured: https://kenang.my/sitemap.xml]
- No e-kad, RSVP or seat finder seen. [measured]
- Weakness: 20 shots per guest is a limit by design; one price RM99 with no free plan. [sourced]

## momenspace (momenspace.my)

- "Untuk majlis perkahwinan di Malaysia. Satukan momen daripada tetamu." [sourced]. Live Wall on every plan, moderation and guest camera filters on Momen. Free has a watermark.
- Bilingual BM and EN. SEO: 7 URLs only (home, features, pricing, faq, terms, privacy, refund, fair use). [measured]
- Weakness: shortest retention in the field (7 to 30 days). Terms say paid plans are "generally not refundable" after meaningful use. [sourced: https://momenspace.my/terms]
- Payment methods: not stated on pages I read. [unverified]

## povkita (povkita.com)

- "YOUR WEDDING. EVERY POINT OF VIEW." Photos, videos and wishes in one album. Ten gallery colour themes, polaroid theme on Premium. [sourced]
- Setup and payment go through WhatsApp: "no payment is taken through this page". [sourced]
- Gallery links are not password protected: "Gallery links are not password-protected". [sourced: FAQ]
- SEO: 6 URLs. [measured]
- Weakness: manual WhatsApp setup, no self-serve checkout; 500 photo cap on Premium; privacy statement is blunt. These are honest contrast points for Indahnya's approval mode and noindex pages (check truth file).

## Gathrly (gathrly.my)

- "Setiap tetamu jadi jurugambar anda." Free for the first 100 photos. [sourced]
- Live Wall, Buku Ucapan, ZIP, QR PDF, guest analytics, 5 themes. Per-tier ticks were not captured in text. [unverified]
- Also an iOS app; terms say purchases go through the App Store (Apple in-app purchase), and Apple handles refunds. [sourced: https://gathrly.my/terms]
- SEO: 40 URLs, a blog in BM and EN (cara kumpul gambar majlis, gambar tetamu vs jurugambar, digital guestbook ideas). [measured]
- Pricing page claims "Photobooth sewa RM400 to RM1,150" as the cost it beats. [sourced; that range is their claim, not verified]

## JomGambar (jomgambar.my)

- "Guestbook & Live Gallery #1 di Malaysia" (self-description). Live projection, stickers and frames, music, a PDF photobook generator, TikTok-style and story views. [sourced]
- Host connects a laptop to a projector over HDMI to show the live wall. [sourced: FAQ]
- Testimonials include one by a "Wedding Planner". [sourced; [unverified]]
- SEO: no sitemap content (109-byte file, no URLs). [measured]
- Weakness: caps (20, 1,000 or 4,000 photos); retention up to 90 days.

## Where Indahnya can honestly beat them

1. Retention: 1 to 2 years kept on paid plans against 7 to 90 days across this group. This is the clearest gap and it matches a real couple fear (photos gone). Confirmed in the truth file.
2. No photo cap on RM59 and RM99, against 500 (povkita Premium), 1,000 to 10,000 (Gathrly, JomGambar) and 20 shots per guest (Kenang).
3. e-kad, RSVP and seat finder in the same product. None of these five shows them.
4. Not honest to claim: cheapest. povkita (RM15 or RM25 promo), Gathrly RM49, JomGambar RM49 and momenspace RM39 all start below RM59.
5. Not honest to claim: more features. Kenang has the film filter and voice notes; JomGambar has a photobook PDF; momenspace and Gathrly have live walls.
