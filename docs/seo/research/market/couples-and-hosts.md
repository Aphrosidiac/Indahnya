## 2. The timeline of a majlis

Not every family does every step; names and order vary by state and family. [third-party: a search summary of Malaysian adat sources, including a Sarawak cultural mapping page and academic PDFs; I did not open them, so treat the sequence as a guide]

| Step | What it is | Why guests take photos or need info |
|---|---|---|
| Merisik | First visit to ask about the bride | Small, family only. Rarely a guest-photo moment [inferred] |
| Bertunang | Engagement, often a feast at the bride's house | Tunang is its own search term: "kad digital tunang" has autocomplete [measured] |
| Akad nikah | The solemnisation, at a mosque, home or hall | Emotional, close family. "Siapa ada gambar masa akad tadi?" appears in a vendor mock chat [sourced: kenanganmajlis.com] |
| Majlis bersanding / resepsi / kenduri | The main feast, can be hundreds of guests, at a dewan, hotel or kampung house | The core use: guest photos, TV slideshow, RSVP, seating [inferred] |
| Majlis menyambut menantu / bertandang | The groom's side reception, or return visit | "majlis bertandang belah lelaki", "majlis bertandang vs sanding" are real autocomplete suggestions [measured]. A second majlis with its own guests; Kenang sells a RM148 two-majlis price for "belah perempuan dan belah lelaki" [sourced: https://kenang.my/] |
| Aqiqah, cukur jambul, doa selamat | Baby and thanksgiving gatherings | "kad digital aqiqah" has autocomplete [measured] |
| Konvokesyen, hari jadi, majlis syarikat | Other occasions | Facts file lists them; low search signal [measured] |

Implication [inferred]: two majlis per wedding is normal (belah perempuan, belah lelaki). The product should handle "second majlis" clearly, because rivals price for it.

Seating: table assignment matters at hotel and dewan receptions with fixed tables. Many kampung kenduri are open-flow, so a table finder is useful for some majlis and irrelevant for others [inferred; I found no source on typical Malaysian seating practice]. A source on catering shows the opposite problem: guests exceed the booked number (a catering association president described a booking for 500 where 1,000 came) [third-party: Kosmo via search summary].

## 3. Pains

### Guest photos
- Photos stay on guests' phones and in WhatsApp groups. Nobody sends them. [third-party: vendor marketing from Kenangan and Kenang; the exact claim is that photos get lost after the majlis]
- WhatsApp quality depends on each sender's setting. HD is optional and per send. [sourced: https://faq.whatsapp.com/759301289012856/]
- The photographer cannot be everywhere. "Photographer anda rakam pelamin. Tetamu anda rakam yang lain." [sourced: https://kenang.my/ marketing copy]
- Couples are tired. Typical vendor framing: pengantin "terkejar" asking guests for photos [sourced: https://kenanganmajlis.com/tentang].
- Evidence that the idea resonates: a viral TikTok of a photographer offering a QR to download pelamin photos: ~280K views, 13K likes, 176 comments [third-party: https://says.com/my/seismik/video-scan-terus-dapat-gambar-atas-pelamin-ramai-puji-cara-kreatif-photographer-ini]. Commenters said local photographers "belum" at this level (paraphrase).

### Invitations (kad)
- Printed cards have no RSVP and cost printing and postage [third-party: Tuan Majlis blog, Kenangan comparison; physical cards still carry sentimental value and respect for elders per a Malaysian article summarised in search].
- Digital cards are shared as a WhatsApp link. Search interest is huge and "free" led: autocomplete shows "kad kahwin digital free", "free online", "free download", "canva", "rsvp free", "buat sendiri" [measured].
- Rivals compete at RM0 (Raikad), RM27.90 (Nak Jemput), RM29 (EkadInvite, Neekah Kita Basic) [sourced].

### RSVP and headcount
- RSVP is for catering and seating. Google Form is the do-nothing tool: autocomplete "rsvp kahwin google form", "contoh rsvp kahwin google form" [measured].
- Over-booking and under-booking food is a real story. Siakap Keli runs a formula article on estimating guests [third-party: https://skreact.siakapkeli.my/articles/buntu-nak-agak-formula-cara-kira-anggaran-jumlah-tetamu-hadir-ke-kenduri-kahwin-nanti]. A catering association head described a 500 booking with 1,000 arrivals at open-door events [third-party: Kosmo via search summary].
- Tuan Majlis frames the RSVP export as "untuk urusan katering dan susunan meja" [sourced].

### Seating
- "nombor meja tetamu", "cari meja tetamu kahwin", "table finder kahwin" have no autocomplete [measured]. Low or unrecognised demand, or the idea is not yet named in BM. [inferred]
- "susun atur meja kenduri kahwin" has one suggestion [measured].

### Wishes and guestbook
- "buku tetamu digital", "ucapan suara kahwin" have no autocomplete [measured]. "guestbook kahwin" does [measured].
- Voice notes are offered by Kenang, Wedibox, GuestCam, tetamu.me and Raikad [sourced]; buyer demand for them is [unverified].

### Money gifts
- Salam kaut by QR is common in e-kad: Tuan Majlis, EkadInvite, Kenang, Kenangan, Neekah Kita all offer it [sourced]. "salam kaut qr" has autocomplete [measured]. Indahnya's e-kad has salam kaut with a DuitNow QR [truth file].

## 4. Buying triggers

[inferred] unless noted.

1. The majlis date is set and invitations are about to go out (6 to 8 weeks before).
2. The kad is bought, and the couple wants the QR on the same link or card.
3. The week or day before: a friend says "nak letak QR tak?" and they search at night.
4. Seeing a viral clip or another couple's QR at a wedding. The SAYS clip is an example [third-party].
5. Cost anchors: a photobooth rental (one rival claims RM400 to RM1,150 [third-party, unverified]) makes RM59 feel small.
6. A vendor (photographer, planner) recommends it.

## 5. Objections and honest answers

| Objection | Reality | Honest answer |
|---|---|---|
| "WhatsApp group kan free" | True, and fine for small groups. Quality depends on each sender; photos mix with chat [sourced, third-party] | Say so. Offer Percuma (RM0, 50 uploads) as the test. Never claim WhatsApp destroys photos |
| "Mahal" / price | RM59 or RM99 against a RM27,332 minimum wedding cost (KPKM-6) [third-party]. Rivals start at RM15 to RM49 for galleries [sourced] | Don't say cheapest. Say what is included and how long photos are kept (check truth file) |
| "Orang tua tak reti scan QR" | No source found. [unverified] | Put the QR on a table card, a standee, and in the WhatsApp link. A card with a short URL as well. Do not promise 100% usage |
| "Takut gambar aurat / privasi" | No Malaysian source found on this. [unverified] Rival pages show passcodes (Kenangan), moderation (Kenang, Indahnya approval mode [truth file]), and povkita states its links are not password-protected [sourced] | Answer with the actual controls the truth file confirms: couple pages noindex, EXIF and GPS removed from shown copies, originals private, approval mode. Passcode: check the truth file |
| "Kalau line dewan teruk?" | Kenang's own FAQ asks it, answer not captured [sourced, unverified]. Wedibox's help says it retries failed uploads three times [third-party] | Facts.md says up to 30 photos at a time. Whether it works without internet is a test prompt in `docs/geo/prompts_v1.csv` (p032) and needs an answer grounded in the code, not a guess |
| "Gambar hilang lepas dua bulan?" | Kenangan: 2 months then paid months and media may be deleted [sourced] | Indahnya's retention is a real edge; verify the numbers first |
| "Nak guna e-kad free dulu" | Raikad is free for now [sourced] | Do not fight it. Be the gallery plus the bundle |
| "Tak percaya brand baru" | Indahnya has no reviews or customer count [truth file] | Be plain: new, run by FF Dev Studio in Terengganu, WhatsApp support, refund promise. Never invent numbers |
