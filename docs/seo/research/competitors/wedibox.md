# Teardown: Wedibox (wedibox.com)

Checked 12 November 2026. Important correction to the brief: Wedibox is a global, English and French product priced in USD. It is not a Malaysian company and has no Malay version. Its structure is the closest in the field to Indahnya's three-plan model.

**Labels.** [measured] fetched today. [sourced] vendor's own page, URL given, checked 12 November 2026. [third-party] others' claims. [inferred] my reading. [unverified] could not confirm.

## What they sell

- One QR code for guest photos and video, RSVP, seating ("Find Your Seat"), digital guestbook, audio guestbook, live slideshow, wedding website, registry links. [sourced: https://www.wedibox.com/]
- Extras: Embed widget for other wedding sites (Wix, Squarespace, Zola and others), partner program for wedding pros, gift purchase, "Wedibox Drops" for photographers and creators. [sourced: same page]

- Upload window starts on the event date, not the purchase date. [sourced: same page]

## Weaknesses and complaints

- Third-party: a search summary of Trustpilot and review pages reported mostly 4 to 5 star reviews, with complaints about video file-size limits, upload failures on busy venue wifi, and paid downloads. Some negative reviews may belong to a different app called "Wedbox", so treat them as unreliable. [third-party, found via WebSearch; Trustpilot itself returned 403 to me]
- Their own help page says photos over 4 MB are compressed before upload and each file retries up to three times on busy wifi. [third-party via search summary; https://app.wedibox.com/help/guest-upload-troubleshooting not fetched by me]
- No Malay, no MYR, no FPX evidence. Prices in USD against a Malaysian ringgit buyer. [measured: currency list]
- Seating and RSVP only at the top plan ($79). [sourced]
- No Malaysian wedding features seen: salam kaut, Waze, majlis types. [measured: not on home or pricing page]

## Where Indahnya can honestly beat them

1. BM-first, ringgit, local payment (FPX and DuitNow QR are the last confirmed methods; do not claim cards or e-wallets yet [truth file]).
2. Price structure is almost the same, so compare in ringgit once at checkout: Indahnya RM59 or RM99 against Wedibox $49 or $79. Do not convert dollars in copy without a dated rate.
3. Indahnya's RM59 plan (and Percuma) includes e-kad, RSVP and the table finder [truth file]. Wedibox needs $79 for RSVP and seating.
4. Malaysian majlis fit (nikah, sanding, kenduri, aqiqah, salam kaut).
5. Not honest to claim: bigger, more proven, more integrations. Wedibox claims 100,000+ weddings and has far more pages.
