# Teardown: Neekah Kita (neekahkita.com)

Checked 12 November 2026. The only Malaysian rival that sells e-kad, RSVP and a QR guest gallery in one place, with a free gallery tier. It also sells printed cards. Found late in the research, via a search for guest-gallery players.

**Labels.** [measured] fetched today. [sourced] vendor's own page, URL given, checked 12 November 2026. [inferred] my reading. [unverified] could not confirm.

## SEO footprint

[measured] 12 November 2026.

- robots.txt: allows all; blocks dashboard, editor, api, masuk, bayar. https://neekahkita.com/robots.txt
- sitemap.xml: 347 URLs. 66 under `/catalogue` (designs), plus about 270 individual sample-invitation pages named by couple (for example `/zikri-arifah`), `/kad-aqiqah`, `/guest-gallery`, `/guest-gallery/contoh`, `/vendor`, `/kelebihan`.
- Pattern: each sample invitation is an indexable page. That is a large long-tail footprint for "kad kahwin digital" terms but thin for gallery terms.
- Languages: BM and EN toggle on the page. [measured]

## Weaknesses

- Gallery caps: 50 photos free, 500 on RM99. Retention: 7 days free, 90 days paid. [sourced]
- No complaints found; Trustpilot and Reddit blocked. [unverified]
- Gallery is a side product of an invitation business; guest-gallery SEO is two pages. [measured]

## Where Indahnya can honestly beat them

1. Same RM99 price point, but unlimited photos against 500, and a 365 day upload window with 730 days kept against 90 days [truth file].
2. Table finder and voice wishes, both built [truth file].
3. A single RM59 tier that already includes e-kad and RSVP, against RM29 plus RM99 for a gallery.
4. Not honest to claim: cheaper e-kad. Their card starts at RM29, and there are printed cards and welcome boards Indahnya does not sell.
